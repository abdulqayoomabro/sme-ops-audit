# Interview guide

Ask in the owner's language. Roman Urdu versions are given because most Pakistani owners answer more honestly in it. Ask about **last week**, not "usually" — real examples beat opinions.

## Opening
- EN: Walk me through yesterday — from opening to closing, what did you and your team do?
- RU: Kal ka din shuru se aakhir tak bataein — aap aur team ne kya kya kaam kiya?

## 1. Leads & sales
- Naye customer kahan se aate hain? (WhatsApp, walk-in, Facebook, referral)
- Pichle hafte kitni inquiries aayin, aur kitni sale mein badlin?
- Agar koi customer reply na kare to follow-up kaun karta hai, kab?

## 2. Orders & delivery
- Order kahan likha jata hai? Ek order shuru se delivery tak kitne haathon se guzarta hai?
- Pichle mahine kitne orders galat ya late gaye? Uska nuqsan kitna hua?

## 3. Inventory & purchasing
- Stock ka hisaab kaise rakhte hain? Aakhri dafa poori ginti kab hui?
- Kabhi aisa hua ke maal khatam ho gaya aur pata nahi chala? Ya maal para para kharab ho gaya?

## 4. Billing, payments & udhaar
- Bill kaise banta hai? Cash, JazzCash/Easypaisa, bank — kis tarah payment aati hai?
- Udhaar kahan likha jata hai? Abhi market mein kitna paisa phansa hua hai? [rough figure]
- Din ke aakhir mein cash aur hisaab milta hai?

## 5. Customer service
- Roz kaun se sawal baar baar aate hain?
- WhatsApp par reply kaun karta hai, aur kitni der mein?
- Shikayat aaye to kahan record hoti hai?

## 6. People
- Haazri kaise lagti hai? Salary par kabhi jhagra hota hai?
- Har banda janta hai ke uski zimmedari kya hai? Kaam kaise assign hota hai?

## 7. Accounts & reporting
- Aaj ka munafa kitna hua — ye aap kitni der mein bata sakte hain?
- Mahine ka hisaab kaun banata hai, kitne din lagte hain?

## 8. Marketing & content
- Kahan post karte hain, kitni dafa? Kaun karta hai?
- Kaise pata chalta hai ke kaun si post se customer aaya?

## Time & cost probes (needed for ROI)
- Is kaam par roz/hafte kitne ghante lagte hain? Kaun karta hai?
- Us bande ki mahana tankhwah taqreeban kitni hai? (for hourly cost: salary ÷ ~208 hours)
- Pichle mahine is wajah se kitna nuqsan hua? (wrong orders, lost udhaar, stock waste)

## Closing
- Agar main sirf ek cheez theek kar sakoon, to kaun si?
- Team mein kaun naye system se sab se zyada pareshan hoga?
