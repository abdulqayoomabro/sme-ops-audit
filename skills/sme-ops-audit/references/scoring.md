# Maturity scoring (0–4)

Score each area on evidence from the interview, not on what the owner hopes.

| Score | Label | What it looks like |
|------:|-------|--------------------|
| 0 | Memory | Lives in someone's head. If they're absent, work stops. |
| 1 | Paper / chat | Registers, diaries or WhatsApp messages. Exists, but can't be searched or totalled. |
| 2 | Spreadsheet | Excel / Google Sheets, updated by hand, often late or inconsistent. |
| 3 | System | One app/software is the source of truth, team uses it daily, reports come out of it. |
| 4 | Connected | Systems talk to each other; routine steps are automated; owner sees a live dashboard. |

## Rules
- A WhatsApp group is **1**, even if it's busy.
- A spreadsheet only one person understands is **1.5** — round down to **1**.
- Software that's bought but not used daily is **1** or **2**, whatever was paid for it.
- Give **3** only if you'd trust its numbers in a dispute.

## Priority
Priority = (4 − score) × pain (1–3, from the owner). Work on the highest priority first. Ties go to the area closest to cash (billing/udhaar, orders).

## Scorecard format

| Area | Score | Pain (1–3) | Priority | Evidence |
|------|------:|-----------:|---------:|----------|
| Billing & udhaar | 1 | 3 | 9 | Udhaar in 2 registers, ~PKR 4 lakh outstanding [Owner said] |
